T4 · Registered print (chosen direction)

T2's corner pockets and squareness + T1's print border and mono caption. No rotation — the pockets register the print like datum points register a part. First pair: paper-beige pockets. Second pair: pencil-blue pockets.

T1 · Taped print

White print border, slight rotation, drafting tape. Captions in mono beneath the print, inside the border — like a labeled photo pinned to the drawing.

T2 · Photo corners

Album-style corner pockets, no rotation. Quieter, more orderly — reads "archival" rather than "working wall."

T3 · Drafting plate

Flat and square — a photographic "detail plate" in the drawing itself. Blue hairline frame, red pin dots, captioned like a figure. The least skeuomorphic of the three; most faithful to the existing design system.
